Financial Formula Constants

These four values drive the Expected Low Net and TCA Low Net columns on the Price Sheet. Change any value, click Save, and every Price Sheet row recomputes immediately.

0.075 = 7.5%
8 = 8 months between PO and rebate
0.90 = 90% of rebates actually collected
0.02 = 2% buffer on Expected Low Net
Formulas (matches your Excel):
Dist Rebate = Dist Redemption × Dist %
Mfr Rebate = Mfr Anchor × Mfr %
Cost of Money = (Dist Rebate + Mfr Rebate + Add'l Funds) × Rate ÷ 12 × Months
Expected Low Net = Dec Cost − CoM − (Dist Rebate × Realization) − ((Mfr Rebate + Add'l Funds) × Realization)
TCA Low Net = Expected Low Net × (1 + Markup)

Import POs from Excel

Upload your PurchasedItemslisting.xlsx file. Each row becomes a PO line. Lines with the same PO # are grouped into one PO. Products are matched by Control #; unknown products auto-create. Re-importing the same PO # overwrites lines but keeps any receipts you've logged.

Lines without a Control # match — pick what to do with each

Map to existing = same product, different packaging. The line will use the existing product's rebate info. Create new = brand new product. You'll fill in rebate info on the Products tab later. Skip = ignore this line during import.

Import Invoices from Chem Prices

Upload your 2026_Chemical_Prices.xlsx file (uses the Chem sheet). Each row becomes a receipt line. Rows sharing an Invoice # are grouped into one PO/receipt.

  • Existing POs (matched by Invoice #) get receipts added to them.
  • Unmatched invoices auto-create POs tagged "from invoice" so you know they came from this import.
  • Unknown products work like the PO import — you decide skip / map / create.
